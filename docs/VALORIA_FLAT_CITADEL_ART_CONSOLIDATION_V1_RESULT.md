## Web / technical research applied

The production wall follows a functional modular-kit strategy rather than repeated identical tower beats. Environment-art breakdowns consistently recommend proving proportions first, using standardized compatible modules, keeping highly distinctive repeated detail out of base modules, and using variants / attachments / material variation to conceal repetition. The current pass therefore replaces the old wall rhythm with hierarchy classes: main gatehouse, quiet curtain groups, buttressed spans, future expansion gates and only two rear watchtowers.

Material consolidation follows URP/PBR rules: stone, wood and earth remain non-metallic; smoothness is constrained to a rough architectural range; normal intensity is bounded rather than amplified blindly. URP decals remain a later optional surface-detail tool rather than a geometry substitute.
