# Valoria — pre-purchase art and financial audit

Date: 2026-09-26  
Repository baseline reviewed: `main` at `6e30d21d2130872b0489fcaca2e05c404477bd10`  
Budget authorized for this block: **0 €**  
Decision scope: pre-purchase research only. **No paid asset was purchased, downloaded into Eldoria, imported into the commercial project, or used to modify Valoria.**

## 1. Decision standard

This audit applies `docs/ELDORIA_VISUAL_BENCHMARK.md`, `docs/VALORIA_ART_ASSET_GATE.md` and `docs/VALORIA_MANUAL_HERO_FRAGMENT_BRIEF.md`.

The question is not whether a package is prettier than the current blockout. The gate is:

> Can this ecosystem materially move Eldoria toward the approved Valoria reference without leaving the full frame looking like a collection of modular prefabs?

The target must support, together, a monumental and vertical fortress, architecture embedded in rock, stepped districts, monumental ruins, bridges/stairs/terraces, organic terrain, believable vegetation, cliffs, a large valley and mountain background, atmospheric depth, blue Valoria identity, a distant territorial Breach, and a premium readable 4X camera.

A package is therefore not accepted just because it has many prefabs, detailed close-up textures, or an attractive marketing scene.

## 2. Executive conclusion

**A) One purchase deserves to pass to an owner authorization phase: Polyart Studio — Skyreach Castle / KINGDOM.**

This is **not** an instruction to buy it and it is **not** a final art approval. It is the only examined commercial family that currently combines, in one coherent visual language and at a relatively low sale floor, all of the following: substantial castle/cathedral architecture, interiors, village/shop content, mountain/cliff environment, multiple trees/bushes/wildflowers, water, LOD support, Unity 6 compatibility and URP compatibility. That breadth is important because it reduces the biggest financial and artistic risk identified in the current Valoria gate: buying one attractive castle and then needing unrelated terrain, vegetation and town packs to complete the frame.

At the public Unity Asset Store price observed on 2026-09-26, Skyreach is **US$74.50 before tax, reduced from US$149**. Unity states that taxes/VAT are calculated at checkout and that displayed prices are exclusive of tax. For a normal Spanish consumer purchase, the current standard VAT rate is 21%; a simple planning estimate is therefore approximately **US$90.14 including 21% VAT at the sale price** or **US$180.29 at the regular price**, before any store currency conversion or account-specific tax treatment. The checkout total remains authoritative.

The authorization should be **sale-sensitive**. At US$74.50, Skyreach has a strong cost-to-coverage argument. At US$149, it remains the strongest ecosystem examined, but the financial advantage narrows and the owner should re-check competitors/sales before paying.

What Skyreach would unlock:
- one dominant, stylistically coherent architectural + mountain + foliage + water family;
- enough castle/cathedral/interior vocabulary to build a much taller, denser Bastion than the current kits;
- enough environmental vocabulary to test Valoria as a whole composition rather than as an isolated fortress;
- a credible source family for the later manually adapted hero fragment.

What Skyreach **does not** unlock automatically:
- the approved Valoria composition;
- a unique hand-authored Bastion silhouette;
- convincing centuries-old damaged palace ruins (a dedicated ruin/destruction family was not verified in the public specification);
- manual architecture-to-rock joins;
- a bespoke Breach biome;
- atmospheric depth, lighting and camera composition;
- proof that the pack survives Eldoria's actual 4X camera without visible repetition.

The last point is the remaining purchase risk. No legitimate free downloadable Skyreach Unity sample was found. Its store gallery/trailer can be evaluated, but an executable pre-purchase camera test could not be performed without acquiring the asset. Therefore **authorization should be for an isolated validation purchase only, not for immediate production adoption**: import into a separate art-test scene, make the three benchmark captures, and reject/refund where the store policy permits if the real game camera exposes unacceptable repetition or style mismatch.

- Unity Standard Asset Store EULA, Single Entity / Extension Asset listing;
- 2.3 GB;
- latest version 1.22, released 2026-02-28;
- original Unity version 2022.3.33;
- explicit Unity 6000.0.62 compatibility: Built-in yes, **URP yes**, HDRP no;
- public specification: **1,567 meshes/prefabs**, 646 textures, 64 px to 4096 px, stated mesh range 2 to 32,000 polygons, LODs present but varying by asset;

- publisher positions it as usable from small forts to giant imperial cities.

The broader Dreamscape line includes Meadows, Mountains, Campsite, Tower and Village. That matters as a future same-family expansion route, but this audit does **not** assume those separate products are owned or required. A related Skyreach mountain listing describes 90+ mountain assets, conifer and broadleaf trees, customizable color/wind, a river/waterfall tool and an auto terrain material, and states that those assets are part of the larger Skyreach Kingdom family.

### Visual evidence reviewed

The current Asset Store listing exposes a 29-image gallery plus trailer. Representative public images show:
- tall light-stone towers rather than squat low-poly blocks;
- steep and varied roof silhouettes;
- cathedral/church massing;
- crenellated walls and vertical focal structures;
- landscaped castle grounds using the same stylized material language;
- enough foliage and cliff vocabulary for the architecture not to stand on a blank plane.

The public imagery is much closer to Eldoria's bright epic-fantasy target than the current procedural Valoria and less overtly low-poly than Synty's base commercial pack. It is still clearly stylized: proportions, foliage and surface treatment are simplified. The important difference is that the geometry does not rely on Synty's highly recognizable faceted/flat-shaded POLYGON look.

A public downloadable pre-purchase Unity sample/demo for Skyreach was **not found**. The trailer and gallery are therefore evidence, not a substitute for the required real-camera validation.

### Benchmark coverage

**Strong coverage**
- monumental castle/cathedral vocabulary;
- vertical towers and rooflines;
- walls, gates and imperial-city-scale modular construction;
- inhabited supporting buildings/shops/interiors;
- cliffs and mountain environment within the same art family;
- trees, bushes and wildflowers;
- water and waterfall-capable environment;
- plausible bright epic-fantasy palette;
- enough breadth to avoid immediately mixing three unrelated commercial packs.

**Conditional coverage**
- stepped districts, stairs, terraces and bridges: the family can support them compositionally, but the public specification does not prove that Valoria-like terracing comes authored out of the box;
- architecture embedded in rock: the pack gives architecture and cliffs, but **the join must be authored**. Dropping a castle prefab on a cliff mesh will fail the benchmark;
- huge valley/mountain background: mountain assets exist, but large-world composition, fog and scale remain scene work;
- Valoria blue identity: must be created through banners/material tuning/lighting; it is not the pack's identity;
- premium 4X readability: promising silhouettes, not yet validated from Eldoria's actual camera.

**Not resolved by the purchase**
- La Brecha territorial biome;
- bespoke ancient disaster damage;
- proven monumental palace ruins;
- hand-resolved roof intersections for the hero architecture;
- unique asymmetry;
- atmospheric perspective;
- final terrain sculpt;
- final lighting/postprocessing.

### Modularity and prefab-collage risk

Risk is **medium**, not low. The pack is explicitly modular. Buying 1,567 assets does not solve authored composition.

To avoid the same failure in a more expensive form:
1. establish the Bastion silhouette with large non-repeating masses before filling details;
2. use modules as raw geometry, not as intact showcase buildings everywhere;
3. manually vary tower heights, roof breaks, buttress rhythm and wall alignment;
4. bury/intersect selected architecture into authored rock where structurally believable;
5. reserve obvious shop presets for lower districts;
6. use cathedral/palace pieces as the basis for a unique Bastion rather than reproducing the demo;
7. author damaged sections and silhouette scars deliberately;
8. enforce Valoria's restrained blue accent language across the entire family.

### Materials, geometry and game-camera expectation

The published 32k upper polygon count and variable LOD support are credible for a modern stylized kit, but they do not prove uniform optimization across a dense 4X city. The architecture appears to retain readable medium-scale forms at pulled-back camera distances: towers, roofs, arches and wall rhythm survive better than micro-surface detail. This is favorable for Eldoria.

The main unresolved distance-camera question is repetition. At 4X distance, repeated roof modules and identical wall/tower units become more visible as a pattern even when close-up materials are good. The pack must pass the exact three-camera test before production promotion.

### Interiors

Interiors are a positive value but not a purchase driver for the current Valoria benchmark. They provide future reuse and imply better architectural completeness, but the 4X exterior frame remains the gate.

### Reviews / known issues

The Asset Store currently shows 17 ratings/reviews and a refund-policy link. Public search did not surface a recurring current Unity 6/URP failure specific to Skyreach. That is useful but not proof of zero issues.

The larger technical risk is not compilation; Unity explicitly marks Unity 6000.0.62 + URP compatible. Eldoria uses 6000.3.23f1, so the pack is in the correct Unity 6/URP family, but exact-patch validation still has to happen on Eldoria's runner.

### Skyreach decision

**Pass to authorization phase, conditional on sale/checkout re-check and immediate isolated 4X camera validation.**

It is the only examined pack that plausibly replaces *several* missing art families at once while staying stylistically near the benchmark.

### The critical distinction: commercial Synty pack vs Unity's Fantasy Kingdom demo

Official Unity demo:
https://unity.com/demos/fantasy-kingdom

The Unity demo is free for educational/reference use under a non-commercial EULA. Unity explicitly says that commercial use of assets contained in the demo requires the appropriate commercial licence to the original Synty package.

Synty explicitly says the downloadable Unity demo **does not include the full commercial POLYGON Fantasy Kingdom pack**.

The premium Unity 6 demo result therefore must not be attributed to the Synty box product alone.

#### What is demonstrably Synty

The commercial pack supplies the base visual vocabulary:
- castle and house modules;
- roofs, towers, walls, stairs, arches, destroyed pieces;
- village/environment props;
- base trees/rocks/paths/foliage;
- characters and set dressing;
- basic FX.

This is a highly coherent and performant low-poly library.

#### What Unity/Incineration added or substantially authored for the demo

Unity documents that the URP demo:
- was created/reworked by Unity's Demo team with Incineration Productions;
- uses **Adaptive Probe Volumes** throughout the world for indirect lighting;
- uses **SpeedTree 10** for rich/diverse vegetation;
- uses **VFX Graph** for butterflies, falling leaves and water splashes;
- uses **Shader Graph** materials;
- uses GPU Resident Drawer;
- uses GPU Occlusion Culling;
- uses Spatial Temporal Post-Processing (STP);
- uses Render Graph and Vulkan optimizations for the mobile version;
- contains an art-directed scene composition specifically built to demonstrate Unity 6.

Synty's own blog is even more revealing: its co-founder recreated a similar vibe using **trees and plants from POLYGON Biomes**. That is direct evidence that the lush demo vegetation should not be treated as something guaranteed by the Fantasy Kingdom commercial pack alone.

Therefore:
- **models:** principally Synty base;
- **materials:** Synty base materials exist, but the Unity showcase uses Shader Graph and scene-specific material work;
- **terrain:** the demo's terrain layout/composition is scene work; it cannot be counted as a commercial-pack feature without inspecting the project;
- **vegetation:** premium demo vegetation is explicitly enriched with SpeedTree 10;
- **lighting:** Unity APV/art-direction work;
- **VFX:** Unity VFX Graph enhancements;
- **post/upscaling/rendering:** Unity 6 systems;
- **composition/camera:** Unity/Incineration authored demo work;
- **additional assets:** at minimum SpeedTree vegetation is additional to the visual result; Synty's own recreation also references POLYGON Biomes vegetation.

### What the free demo proves — and what it does not

It proves that **Synty geometry can participate in a very attractive Unity 6 URP scene** when surrounded by excellent terrain composition, high-quality foliage, lighting, shaders, effects and technical optimization.

It does **not** prove that purchasing POLYGON Fantasy Kingdom gives Eldoria that look.

That distinction changes the financial result: the real cost of matching the Unity demo is not US$175. It is US$175 **plus** substantial environment-art labor and possibly additional commercial vegetation/terrain content. No invented dollar amount is assigned to that unpriced gap.

### Visual fit to Valoria

The base pack remains visibly Synty POLYGON: faceted, simplified, flat/chunky forms are part of its identity. At a pulled-back 4X camera that can be very readable and performant, but the style is more overtly low-poly/cartoon than the Eldoria benchmark.

**Strengths**
- exceptional city/prop breadth;
- castle + village + characters + siege/life props in one visual family;
- destroyed architecture pieces are explicitly included;
- low-poly geometry is favorable for dense mobile scenes;
- modular stairs/roofs/walls make vertical city construction straightforward;
- demonstrated Unity 6 URP potential.

**Weaknesses for Eldoria**
- recognizable Synty visual signature competes with Eldoria's intended identity;
- close and medium-distance geometry is less rich than the approved reference;
- the premium Unity demo's vegetation/lighting/material depth is external production work, not a box feature;
- buying Synty at the current sale price costs more than twice Skyreach while still requiring a major finishing layer;
- mixing richer non-Synty cliffs/vegetation later can produce exactly the pack-collage problem this audit is meant to avoid.

### Demo execution status

The Unity demo is legally available free for non-commercial educational/reference use. It was **not imported into Eldoria**.

It was also **not executed locally in this audit**, because the current agent environment does not provide an authenticated Asset Store package-acquisition path into a disposable Unity project. The home Unity runner can execute repository-controlled jobs, but this block did not authorize attaching a non-commercial third-party sample to the commercial repository or changing the owner's Asset Store account.

Consequently, no falsely claimed local three-camera screenshots are recorded. The analysis uses Unity's official published views and technical documentation. A future isolated non-commercial copy outside Eldoria could be inspected on the owner's PC if desired.

### Known issues / maintenance

The Synty Unity changelog shows a mature pack with repeated fixes for shaders, collisions, missing materials/faces, transforms, FX and demo cleanup. These are mostly historical maintenance rather than a current blocker.

A historical Unity 6 beta RenderDoc crash was associated with the Fantasy Kingdom URP scene. It is not treated as a reason to reject the commercial Synty pack, because it concerned tooling/demo interaction in beta-era Unity rather than a demonstrated current Eldoria runtime defect.

### Synty decision

**Do not authorize as Valoria's primary art purchase at the current evidence level.**

It is a very strong production library, but the specific visual result that makes it tempting is largely the result of Unity/Incineration's finishing work. For Eldoria, the pack is more likely to become an expensive foundation that still needs another landscape/vegetation/look-development layer.

**Decision: reject as the sole Valoria foundation.** It remains a good value castle kit in isolation, but not the lowest-total-cost path to this benchmark.

Because those are a mixture of labor, engine features and potentially extra assets, assigning a fake fixed bundle price would be misleading. The known cash floor is already more than twice Skyreach's sale price before the finishing layer.

Use only if a Skyreach art test proves the architecture itself is not rich enough and the project consciously accepts a more sculpted/semi-realistic material direction.
