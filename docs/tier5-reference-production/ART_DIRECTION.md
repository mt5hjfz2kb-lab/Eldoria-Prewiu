# Six individually referenced assets — production brief

Owner's supplied images are authoritative. Preserve their exact bytes and SHA256. Do not introduce an alternative castle, industrial factory or ornamental character. Sources stay outside Unity until separately reviewed.

## Artistic checks
- Lyra: human face/ears, warm olive-brown skin, dark hair, practical muted green cape and brown leather, full body and bow. Inspect hands, legs, cape intersections and bow clearance from eight directions. A neutral useful production pose is preferred; a fused or unriggable body is a rejection, even if its front image is attractive.
- Bastion V A: dominant square rear keep, lower square towers, limestone and dark blue slate, connected courtyard and gateway. Preserve the reference mass hierarchy; do not enlarge the complex or substitute round towers.
- Sawmill V B: long pitched blue roof, open timber working bays, simple stone service room, organized visible logs and restrained timber handling frame.
- Barracks V A: open military training courtyard is the defining empty space. Do not fill it with opaque roof/rock geometry; subordinate the square watchtowers to the functional building.
- Granary V: clear barn entrance and upper loading doors with simple hoist, limited tower and lean-to storage. Keep sacks readable without multiplying ornaments.
- Quarry V: one compact roofed workshop, restrained hoist, visible stone processing wheel and small loading area. Do not generate an excavation mountain or scatter an oversized field of stone blocks.

## Review and finishing
Review source renders before processing the next paid asset. Retain rejected attempts and reasons. If a source fails, change the causal input or settings rather than automatically rerun an identical request. A changed request iteration is explicit and keeps the previous task.

Use existing Eldoria canonical decimation and image optimization with provisional individual triangle budgets: Lyra 30K, Bastion 45K, sawmill 25K, barracks 35K, granary/quarry 25K. Preserve UVs and materials; compare optimized renders to raw before approval. These numbers are not physical-device performance certificates.

Each attempt preserves original reference, generation configuration, submission JSON/task ID, raw GLB, balances and source hash. Approved source finishing adds optimized GLB, packed editable Blend, eight-angle renders and a reference comparison. Keep source visual acceptance separate from Unity import, rigging and physical mobile performance.
