## Gameplay role

Granero unlocks at **Bastion III** and introduces food/survival economy. It is the next missing DEDICATED FUNCTIONAL building after Aserradero and Cuartel.

It must read instantly as food/storage infrastructure from the official camera without becoming a fortress, manor or generic house.

## Approved district / plot intent

District: **West Growth / food-survival edge**, connected to the existing west economic/rebuilders side.

The Granary may replace current placeholder/support housing inside its reserved plot; those houses are not authoritative gameplay topology.

Provisional production anchor for integration:
- centre: approximately **(-17.2, 0.34, -3.0)** in the current Master Envelope;
- principal face: **south/front toward the readable lower route**;
- east flank kept visually open enough to preserve route/readability from the official camera;
- gameplay hotspot/click volume remains independent from the visual mesh.

### Building envelope

Target visual footprint:
- width: **4.1–4.5**
- depth: **3.4–3.8**
- height: **3.7–4.4**

Reserved long-term upgrade envelope:
- width: **5.4**
- depth: **4.8**
- maximum intended height: **5.8**

The first asset must fit inside the target footprint. The larger envelope is reserved so later food-storage tiers can add lean-tos, loft capacity, awnings, grain handling and repair/prosperity detail without moving roads.

## Visual identity

Single compact medieval-fantasy granary / food storehouse for Valoria.

Required read:
- robust stone lower plinth/base;
- warm timber upper/storage body;
- steep restrained slate/shingle roof;
- one clear recessed front loading door;
- one secondary side loading/storage cue;
- visible grain/food identity through architecture: ventilation slots, raised/dry storage logic, small sack/crate handling area;
- asymmetrical but compact mass;
- restrained Valoria blue heraldic accent only;
- rebuilt-after-catastrophe character, not pristine palace architecture.

Avoid:
- towers;
- battlements;
- gatehouse silhouette;
- giant silo cylinder;
- windmill blades;
- open market stall as the main building;
- sprawling farm complex;
- disconnected props floating around the asset;
- large rock pedestal;
- fused district/diorama.

## Tripo exact-input requirements

The approved Tripo input must be:
- **one single isolated 3/4 image**;
- neutral light-grey background;
- entire building visible with comfortable margins;
- camera slightly elevated, showing south/front + east side;
- no text, labels, arrows, scale bars, contact sheet or multiple views;
- no surrounding city, terrain, trees or people;
- no dramatic fog or strong cast shadow hiding geometry;
- grounded on a minimal neutral base plane only if needed for legibility.

The image exists to maximize image-to-3D reconstruction quality, not as concept-art presentation.
