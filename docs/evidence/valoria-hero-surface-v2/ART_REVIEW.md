# Valoria gate — V2 art review

**Decision: REWORK as final library anchor; retain V2 as the best editable candidate.** The V1 original stays immutable at commit `4d9ac9eb`. The visual comparison is `art-source/valoria/production/first-hero-gate-v1/quality-v2/before-after-matched.png`, assembled from two real Cycles renders of the **same loaded Blender scene** with identical camera, lighting, color management, 32 samples and resolution. The original reference `references/VALORIA_APPROVED_VISUAL_REFERENCE.jpg` was opened visually: compact pale masonry, aged but readable courses, deep portals, blue/gold heraldry and warm points of light. Its landscape is outside this isolated asset's scope.

The accepted interventions remove 36 square face patches masquerading as corner quoins, reshape 775 existing ashlar meshes with irregular arrises/recesses and more varied bevels, integrate 16 two-tier box crenels into single shaped stones, resolve the two continuous crown slabs into ten fitted coping stones, and replace overly uniform stone/oak maps with authored base-color/roughness/normal bitmaps. The footprint, twin square towers, arch, rastrillo, doors, banners, flame positions and lighting stay the same. The first high-contrast surface pass was **REJECTED** because it produced repeated rust speckles and marble-like streaks; the subdued second pass was retained. The glTF flat-arch regression was corrected. A destructive LOD2 collapse was **REJECTED**; only visually safe LOD1 is retained.

At hero view V2 reads as bonded masonry rather than V1's pasted-on corner squares; individual courses have more depth and the crowns are less toy-like. The wood is more weathered. The fixed strategic view preserves the clear gate silhouette. Still, the courses are conspicuously regular, the merlons are blocky, and the stone surface lacks the localized hand-worked damage and material richness of the approved scene. The metal and fire remain simple. My subjective assessment moves from the owner's ~5.5/10 baseline to approximately **6.5–7/10** in Blender. This is a meaningful improvement, **not** a defensible 7.5–8/10 or a final ART PASS. A focused hand-authored stone pass, especially on the arch, parapets and corner transitions, is the next artistic action; do not proliferate variants or substitute lighting changes.

Actual tools: Blender 4.2.5 mesh/material editing and Cycles, NumPy/Pillow original PBR maps, GLB reimport and real LOD renders, the existing `valoria-art-brain/bridge.py` check and historical source review, GitHub Actions for reproducible production. BlenderMCP executed in V1 only; the V2 bounded mesh edits run directly in Blender. The historical Ollama/Qwen3-VL adapter was inspected but localhost Ollama in this container returned connection refused, so no Qwen judgment exists for this exact V2 image. This is not an Eldoria access failure: free local Qwen3-VL image inference subsequently passed in the project workflow `.github/workflows/valoria-qwen-vision-runtime.yml`, run `38150397153` (job `114502469645`) on `ops/valoria-art-brain-runtime-recovery-20261011`. Its success is a capability proof, not an art verdict for this gate. No CC0 donor was used; the maps are original deterministic code, avoiding an unrelated or mismatched texture. No paid credits were spent.
