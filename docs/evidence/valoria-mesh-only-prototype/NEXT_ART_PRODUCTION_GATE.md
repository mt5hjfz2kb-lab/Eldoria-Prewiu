## Art acceptance (mandatory human/vision review, NOT inferred from CI)
FAIL if any of these are visible at gameplay camera:
- floating/disconnected buildings, walls, bridge, roads or staircases;
- dominant box-like wall/tower shapes, obvious repeating cottage clones, angular suspended terrain slabs;
- materials without believable scale/variation or visually inconsistent style;
- empty surroundings, crude vegetation, unclear fortress silhouette;
- materially inferior resemblance to canonical Valoria art direction.
PASS only on reference-matched whole-frame and close-frame captures after corrections. Record reviewer observations and explicit remaining issues.
Never turn a successful render, image-size check, polygon budget, material count, or Blender export into an art PASS.
