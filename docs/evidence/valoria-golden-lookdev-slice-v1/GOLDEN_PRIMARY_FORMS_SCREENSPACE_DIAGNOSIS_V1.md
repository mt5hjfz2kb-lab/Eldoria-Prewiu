## Canonical reference reading

The reference does not derive its quality from micro-detail alone. Its premium read comes from a hierarchy of large and medium forms:

1. architecture has a readable structural base, body and termination;
2. openings are deep voids rather than decals or shallow cuts;
3. towers and walls interlock with terrain instead of sitting on a flat platform;
4. bridge/arch structures show an obvious load path;
5. geological masses are broad, continuous and eroded, not sheets or repeated boulders;
6. vegetation is sparse enough for each canopy silhouette to read;
7. asymmetry exists at the level of massing, not only surface noise;
8. materials react to real plane changes generated by geometry.

## LOWER GATE — required primary-form read

### Existing failure to eliminate
- straight extruded tower/wall planes dominate;
- repeated crenellation/block rhythm reads kit-like;
- central access lacks enough screen-space depth;
- base/plinth does not visibly grow from or bury into terrain;
- left/right balance is too mechanically mirrored;
- detail is asked to create richness that the massing does not supply.

### Source01 design requirement
From the official gameplay view the Gate must read in this order:

**buried foundation → two unequal tower masses → deep central void → stepped upper connector → selective crown rhythm**

Hard checks:
- tower feet visibly widen/batter into the bank;
- tower bodies are not simple cuboids in silhouette;
- left/right tower profiles differ without losing the paired-gate identity;
- arch depth creates at least three readable planes: front ring, passage/reveal, rear darkness;
- wings descend into terrain instead of terminating as floating rectangular slabs;
- crowns use a few large terminations rather than a dense row of equal small blocks;
- the Gate retains its locked macro center and gateway function.

Reject if the clay reads as:
- two boxes + arch;
- castle-kit crenellation strip;
- flat facade;
- decorative gate placed on terrain rather than embedded in it.

## BRIDGE — required primary-form read

### Existing failure to eliminate
- deck and supports read as assembled technical pieces;
- arch/support relationship does not communicate load;
- banks do not convincingly receive the structure;
- parapets and deck remain visually separate from support logic.

### Source01 design requirement
From the official view the Bridge must read in this order:

**bank burial → abutment/spring → deep arch → cambered deck → restrained edge/parapet**

Hard checks:
- visible spring points and abutments;
- arch has real barrel/underside thickness;
- deck is not a flat rectangular slab;
- bridge ends disappear structurally into both banks;
- parapet follows the deck rather than reading as an independent rail kit;
- support mass is subordinate to the crossing silhouette.

Reject if the clay reads as:
- slab on blocks;
- thin arch decal;
- road strip with rails;
- support pieces placed in front of terrain.

## CLIFF / GROUND / SHORE — required primary-form read

### Existing failure to eliminate
- broad designed sheet/grade appearance;
- pasted local islands or aprons;
- geology represented by donor boulder density;
- shoreline as a clean material boundary;
- architecture contacting a surface rather than entering geological mass.

### Source01 design requirement
The bank must read as **one geological system** with:
- broad beds and terraces;
- erosion-driven plane changes;
- embedded architectural foundations;
- a continuous dry bank → broken rock/soil → wet shelf → water sequence;
- no visible perimeter of a local patch;
- no single flat beige field dominating the crop.

Large form first: one or two broad geological breaks are preferable to many small rocks.

Reject if:
- any large terrain surface reads like a sheet;
- a local apron boundary is visible;
- rocks appear to be scattered props used to hide a flat base;
- the wet shelf is a painted ring.

## VEGETATION — required primary-form read

### Existing failure to eliminate
- branch skeleton / technical tree construction;
- cone-like repeated silhouette;
- density used as concealment;
- weak trunk contact.

### Source01 design requirement
Only a few trees should be necessary. Each must show:
- one dominant canopy mass;
- secondary offset lobes;
- asymmetrical outer contour;
- visible trunk-to-ground contact;
- different height/width rhythm from the other trees.

Reject if:
- canopy reads as stacked cones;
- branch structure dominates over foliage mass;
- three trees look like scaled copies;
- foliage obscures structural defects.

## Anti-loop decision tree

If source01 fails:

- Gate failure → rebuild Gate primary masses; do not decorate.
- Bridge failure → rebuild structural section/load path; do not add props.
- Terrain failure → rebuild continuous bank topology; do not add rocks.
- Vegetation failure → rebuild canopy masses; do not increase density.
- Material failure with geometry >=4 → adjust surface relationship only.
- Contact failure with good isolated assets → change actual burial/intersection form.

A second iteration is valid only if it changes the dominant failing primary form materially.
