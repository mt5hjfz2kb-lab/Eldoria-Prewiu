## Screen-space diagnosis

### Silhouette language
The target uses interlocking, stepped and eroded silhouettes with clear primary/secondary hierarchy. The current crop still exposes long straight substrate edges, broad planar masses and repeated rectangular architectural terminations. Result: prototype/kit read.

### Rock stratification / cliff breakup
The target reads as larger geological beds broken by secondary fractures and embedded architecture. The current crop alternates between faceted inherited cliffs and added local boulders. The local rocks improve contact but never become the dominant continuous geological language.

### Architecture edge hierarchy
The target has thick base/plinth transitions, recessed openings, structural returns, selective cornice/cap emphasis and non-uniform edge wear. Lower Gate and Bridge remain too reliant on simple tower/deck/parapet profiles with repeated small blocks. Their material cannot look premium while the silhouette remains mechanically simple.

### Gate massing/profile quality
The macro position is correct and locked, but the visible lower tower/plinth/arch edge needs a stronger hierarchy: battered foundation, deep opening lip, asymmetric lower shoulder, real depth around the arch and a contact profile that enters terrain instead of sitting on it.

### Bridge support/contact quality
The current bridge reads primarily as deck + parapets + simplified support. The target requires visible load path: spring points, tapered abutments/piers, arch depth, underside thickness and believable burial into the banks.

### Ground breakup
The inherited beige field remains too broad and uniform. Source02 proves local patches cannot hide it without visible boundaries. The replacement therefore must be continuous within the authorized micro-environment and derive its breakup from form (grade, drainage, erosion, stone/soil transition), not cosmetic polygons.

### Shoreline transition
The target needs a depth sequence: dry bank -> broken rock/soil -> darker wet shelf -> water. The current shoreline is too flat and reads as a material boundary. The new base must make the wet contact follow topography.

### Vegetation silhouette
The retained/source02 conifer direction is acceptable at gameplay distance, but the slice needs fewer, stronger asymmetrical silhouettes with visible trunk grounding and non-conical canopy rhythm.

### Material readability
Golden Surface V2 is retained as the validated starting surface stack. It failed only because large-scale form dominated it. New geometry must provide enough plane hierarchy for stone/rock/ground roughness and normals to react to light.

### Contact depth
The target uses recess, burial, overlap and shadow generated by real form. Current contacts often rely on adjacency or shallow patching. Gate plinth, bridge spring/abutment and shoreline need actual depth.

### Lighting interaction
Lighting is not the blocker and remains locked for the source decision. The new source must create readable light-facing, side and occluded planes under the existing recipe rather than relying on stronger grading/fog.

## Reauthoring design

One integrated source will author only the screen-visible Golden micro-environment:

1. **Lower Gate structural edge profile**
   - deep arch lip / voussoir band;
   - battered lower plinth shoulders;
   - selective cap/cornice hierarchy;
   - asymmetrical terrain burial at tower feet.

2. **Bridge structural edge/support**
   - cambered deck edge;
   - thick arch ring;
   - tapered spring/abutment masses;
   - underside depth and bank burial.

3. **Continuous substrate**
   - two continuous banks separated by the water channel;
   - designed terrace-to-cliff transition;
   - broad geological beds + secondary fracture planes;
   - no floating apron/perimeter patch.

4. **Ground**
   - upper walkable shelf carried by the same bank mesh;
   - mid-frequency grade/erosion breakup;
   - Golden Surface V2 reuse where compatible.

5. **Shore**
   - topographic wet shelf and embedded shore rock;
   - no painted ring or rectangular strip.

6. **Vegetation**
   - three bounded asymmetrical conifers only;
   - trunk grounding and canopy hierarchy.
