## Concrete transferable lessons

- The 2.5D technique is not an automatic aesthetic benefit. A shared scene is only useful if objects are spatially compatible. Existing hero portraits include their own backgrounds, scale and illumination: placing them on a scenic gallery does not make them a continuous environment. Treat such portraits as genuine framed dossiers, or author compatible alpha foregrounds separately. Never claim the latter from a flattened portrait.
- The new true T1/T2 alpha archer is correct but too small and dark at phone scale. Allocate a real large silhouette area, ensure material contrast, and use a coherent footprint. The old thumbnail had better immediate recognition.
- Reduced decorative frames did not automatically improve hierarchy. Preserve clear readable cell boundaries, distinct levels of information and emphasis on the commander and actual CTA. Do not remove definition simply to reduce ornament.
- Keep source-pinned actual native images and same-state paired independent scoring. Compilation and zero recorded defects are functional evidence, not art approval. Validate new Unity meta GUIDs as exactly 32 hex chars before import.
