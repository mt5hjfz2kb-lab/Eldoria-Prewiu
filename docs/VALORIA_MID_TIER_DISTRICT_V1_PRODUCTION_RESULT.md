## Rollout boundary
The family is promoted to production for the certified Mid/Lower sector and for future **selective** compatible placements. It is deliberately not stamped across every Valoria parcel: the certified sector proof showed that whole-city repetition would expose the same red-roof family too strongly.

Therefore “apply everywhere it improves” means:
1. promote the certified three-parcel sector now;
2. reuse the family selectively where its silhouette/function fits;
3. do not replace specialized/civic/hero architecture merely because a Mid-Tier piece can physically fit;
4. expand broader city coverage only after another architectural family or specialized vocabulary is certified.

This preserves the visual gain without turning one successful family into a new repetition problem.
