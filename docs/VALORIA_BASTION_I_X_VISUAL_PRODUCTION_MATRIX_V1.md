## Non-negotiable rule

Valoria starts at **Bastion I with NO completed Sawmill**.

The Sawmill is the player's first construction/reconstruction action. At initial state only its plot / damaged footprint / construction need may be visible. Completed functional Sawmill architecture appears only after the authoritative construction state completes.

More generally:

> A building may have reserved space, foundations, ruin cues or a construction state before unlock, but completed architecture and gameplay interaction must not appear early.

## Bastion art-tier strategy

Do not author ten unrelated Bastion models for levels I–X.

Canonical production strategy for the first arc:

- **Tier A — Ruined:** Bastion I–IV.
- **Tier B — Recovered:** Bastion V–IX.
- **Tier C — Fortified:** Bastion X–XIV planning band.

Intermediate level feedback should use additive/removable authored states where useful:
- scaffolds and construction materials;
- repaired wall/roof sections;
- tower or roof additions;
- banners and lights;
- courtyard treatment;
- wall completion;
- population/activity;
- props and stockpiles;
- controlled ruin removal.

Every Bastion ascent needs at least one perceivable Valoria change, but not every ascent needs a full new major mesh.

## Functional-building art-state contract

For each important functional building, production should explicitly identify:

1. **Reserved plot / pre-unlock state**
2. **Construction state**
3. **Early functional state**
4. **Developed state**
5. **Advanced/prestige state only where justified**

Small/support buildings may need fewer states. The maximum footprint/height envelope must be reserved early enough that later upgrades never require moving canonical roads, camera bounds or neighboring core plots.

## Required production registry fields for every future building

Before final geometry is locked, record:

- canonical unlock Bastion / chapter event;
- district / plot owner;
- maximum footprint and height;
- what the plot looks like before unlock;
- construction-state requirement;
- number and identity of major art tiers;
- whether intermediate levels use additive modules/dressing only;
- gameplay hotspot timing;
- official-camera clickability/readability;
- dependency on roads/walls/terrain;
- compatibility with Bastion 25–35 master envelope.

Unknown fields remain planning gaps; they are not permission to invent progression in art.

## Relationship to current visual-production reset

Current art production remains:

`target → locked camera → approved full-scene blockout → representative family → ART SOURCE gate → Unity matched-camera VISUAL gate → structural visual slice → scale production`

The Lower Gate family is the current representative family. This matrix must not cause Bridge, Road, Stair, Bastion or progression-state art to begin before the current family gate closes.

Once the structural visual slice is proven, Bastion I should become the first complete player-facing progression state assembled from the proven family language. The first Sawmill construction sequence should then become the first functional building progression proof.

## First progression slice after structural visual proof

Target sequence:

`Bastion I initial ruined Valoria → Sawmill need/plot → construction → completed Sawmill → visible local city-growth feedback → world trip/reward/return`

This preserves the web vertical slice's core loop while replacing placeholder presentation with the new production-quality Valoria language.

## External playtest gate

After the first production-quality Bastion I slice exists, test with a player who has not followed development. Verify that they can determine without coaching:

- what structure is the Bastion;
- that the Sawmill is **not built yet** at the start;
- what they are expected to do first;
- where the world/exit route is;
- that Valoria visibly changes after construction;
- why the city growth matters to the next action.

Do not reduce the test to “do you like the art?”.
