# Valoria Progression Map v1

Date: 2026-09-28  
Status: **ACTIVE PLANNING BASELINE**

## Purpose

Define how the same Valoria grows visually and spatially from the current Bastion I–X arc toward the long-term planning range of approximately Bastion 25–35.

This is a **visual/spatial progression map**, not a balance table. It does not invent final resource costs, timers or unlock levels for systems that are not yet designed.

## 1. Two progression rhythms

Valoria uses two simultaneous rhythms:

### Micro progression
Every Bastion level should produce at least one noticeable feedback beat:
- repair;
- construction;
- scaffold change;
- roof/tower addition;
- banner/light/prop density;
- road improvement;
- plot occupation;
- wall completion;
- visible activity.

The player should not advance a Bastion level and see literally nothing change.

### Macro progression
Large city/art transformations happen at milestone bands.

Production should avoid 35 wholly separate city meshes. Use:
- stable master terrain/circulation;
- building tier variants;
- additive/removable modules;
- district activation;
- material/prop states;
- ruin/repaired variants.

## 2. Arc I — Bastion 1–10

The first ten levels require denser visual feedback because they form the playable prologue/tutorial arc.

### Bastion 1 — Refuge / ruin
- smallest active footprint;
- damaged central stronghold;
- main route readable but rough;
- very sparse occupied plots;
- large portions of future Valoria read as ruin, rock or inaccessible ground;
- city feels vulnerable.

### Bastion 2 — First functional foothold
- first meaningful functional construction appears;
- scaffolding / work activity increases;
- one damaged area begins visibly stabilizing.

### Bastion 3 — Settlement identity
- first residential/civilian signs become clear;
- a repaired roof/wall/tower beat changes silhouette;
- lower district feels inhabited rather than camp-like.

### Bastion 4 — Route consolidation
- main street / plaza treatment improves;
- another plot or defensive segment becomes visibly active;
- damaged edges remain dominant outside the kernel.

### Bastion 5 — Recovered stronghold
- first major Bastion art-tier change;
- central keep reads substantially more complete;
- defensive outline improves;
- early city footprint is still compact.

### Bastion 6 — Organized city
- economic/military areas read as intentional districts;
- more permanent materials replace provisional repair language;
- civilian density increases.

### Bastion 7 — Upper growth
- upper plot / civic presence becomes more important;
- Bastion gains a clear architectural addition rather than only props;
- city begins to hint at later prestige.

### Bastion 8 — Fortification
- walls/gates/towers gain another completion beat;
- active city boundary becomes clearer;
- routes remain visually open and clickable.

### Bastion 9 — Pre-finale expansion
- last major Arc-I empty/ruined area near the kernel gains purpose;
- stronger banners, lighting and population cues;
- city looks prepared for a larger role.

### Bastion 10 — Arc-I complete
- second major Bastion art-tier change;
- early Valoria reads as a coherent fortified settlement;
- kernel is mature but **most long-term city envelope is not yet fully occupied**;
- next growth should visibly require camera exploration.

## 3. Long-range progression bands

### 11–15 — First city expansion
Spatial:
- West or East Growth District meaningfully extends beyond the Arc-I kernel;
- camera pan becomes necessary to inspect all active buildings;
- additional plots open rather than cramming the core.

Visual:
- stronger permanent construction;
- first mature district identity;
- Bastion tier advances around the band milestone;
- walls extend to include new urban ground.

Target occupied share of eventual authored envelope: **roughly 45–55%**, provisional until graybox.

### 16–20 — Mature fortified settlement
Spatial:
- both lateral wings have active functions;
- upper civic/government territory grows;
- secondary connections may open if proven readable.

Visual:
- fewer visible ruins in active districts;
- denser civilian life;
- more deliberate landscaping/road finish;
- Bastion becomes a regional-power silhouette.

Target occupied share: **roughly 60–70%**.

### 21–25 — Regional capital
Spatial:
- most major district classes are active;
- defensive ring approaches mature form;
- reserved late-game plots become visible as meaningful destinations.

Visual:
- civic/government architecture gains prominence;
- larger banners/statues/monuments may appear when earned by systems;
- richer streets and population signals;
- city reads as a capital rather than a repaired fortress.

Target occupied share: **roughly 75–85%**.

### 26–30 — High-status late game
Spatial:
- long-term lateral footprint is nearly fully used;
- high-tier special structures occupy previously reserved plots;
- transport between visual zones remains simple enough for mobile navigation.

Visual:
- advanced Bastion tier;
- monumental walls/towers/civic silhouettes;
- high density without destroying route readability;
- prestige lighting and decoration.

Target occupied share: **roughly 85–95%**.

### 31–35 — Monumental / prestige Valoria
Spatial:
- master envelope is substantially complete;
- remaining reserved spaces are prestige/special-system territory, not basic utility overflow.

Visual:
- final planned Bastion prestige tier;
- strongest skyline;
- mature civic and defensive identity;
- unique earned monuments / alliance or world-achievement visual language where applicable;
- city should feel like the same place as Bastion 1, transformed over time.

Target occupied share: **up to the approved production envelope**.

## 4. Bastion art-tier strategy

Do not create 35 unrelated Bastion models.

Recommended production architecture:
- **Tier A — Ruined:** levels 1–4
- **Tier B — Recovered:** levels 5–9
- **Tier C — Fortified:** levels 10–14
- **Tier D — Expanded:** levels 15–19
- **Tier E — Regional:** levels 20–24
- **Tier F — Capital:** levels 25–29
- **Tier G — Monumental:** levels 30–35

Within each tier, level feedback can use additive pieces and state changes:
- scaffolds;
- repaired sections;
- towers;
- roof segments;
- banners;
- windows/lights;
- walls;
- courtyard treatment;
- statues/ornament;
- population/props.

Exact tier breakpoints remain adjustable if gameplay later chooses another level cap. The important production rule is **few authored major tiers + meaningful intermediate states**.

## 5. Other building upgrade strategy

Each functional building should generally use fewer major art tiers than Bastion.

Recommended default:
- foundation/construction state;
- early functional state;
- developed state;
- advanced state;
- prestige/final state where justified.

Not every building needs all five. Small/support buildings may use 2–3.

A building upgrade must never require moving the road or another core plot because the maximum envelope was not reserved.

## 6. District unlock strategy

Progression should expand outward rather than only replacing buildings in place.

Preferred rhythm:
- 1–5: Kernel only;
- 6–10: Kernel densifies + first adjacent reservation becomes legible;
- 11–15: first lateral district expansion;
- 16–20: opposite lateral district + upper civic expansion;
- 21–25: defensive/civic capital expansion;
- 26–30: special/advanced plots;
- 31–35: prestige completion.

Which side unlocks first is a gameplay decision and should be chosen based on actual building sequence, not aesthetics alone.

## 7. Construction-state presentation

When practical, important upgrades should pass through visible construction states:
- scaffold;
- materials/wood piles;
- workers/activity cues;
- partial wall/roof;
- completion reveal.

Do not require bespoke construction animation for every minor building level. Reserve richer transitions for:
- Bastion tier changes;
- district unlocks;
- major system buildings;
- milestone fortifications.

## 8. Regression questions for every new feature

Before adding a new city building/system, answer:

1. At what progression band does it become visible?
2. Which district owns it?
3. What is its maximum footprint/height?
4. Does it need a dedicated plot from Bastion 1?
5. What does the plot look like before unlock?
6. What are its art tiers?
7. Can it remain clickable at all official zooms and pan positions?
8. Does it force a camera-bound or topology change?
9. Does it still fit the level-25–35 city plan?

If these are unknown, do not lock production geometry.
