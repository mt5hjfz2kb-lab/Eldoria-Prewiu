## Proof hypothesis

A small authored modular family can raise quality more effectively than another placement/material pass if it shares one consistent vocabulary:
- warm natural stone foundation and wall modules;
- dark structural timber;
- warm plaster infill;
- restrained blue slate/heraldic accents;
- dark iron hardware;
- beveled silhouette edges with weighted-normal shading;
- deterministic pivots and scale.

The first family contains only:
1. Aserradero proof asset;
2. one wall module;
3. one gate module.

No Cuartel/Granero expansion is allowed until the three-asset proof is captured in Unity and shows a clear benchmark jump.

## Applied external workflow research

Current modular-environment guidance consistently emphasizes fixed module dimensions/pivots, bevels to avoid razor-sharp specular edges, weighted normals for hard-surface shading, and large-form readability before decorative detail. Professional architectural workflows also emphasize reusable trim/material systems rather than per-object ad hoc surfacing.
