## Audit before modifying meshes

The six source modules contain one fused mesh each. Candidate upward-facing triangles do not distinguish walking floor from parapets, roof or rock. The connector source's actual central upward-facing surface is ~0.21–0.22 GLB units across at its ends, approximately 1.72–1.84 Unity units at the review scale 8.2; this **does not establish unobstructed width**. All three longitudinal circulation modules have low candidate floor at source +Z and high candidate at -Z; the review orients each at 180° yaw. A socket is a placement hypothesis until verified by walk-surface probes and visual continuity.

## Verdict and exact missing rule

**TECH PASS** for the isolated import/render/mesh/raycast checks, **INTERFACE FAIL** for all six proposed physical joins (all six retain `FUNCTION CERTIFIED`), **VISUAL / URBAN FAIL**. The failure does not justify a seventh family or more rotations. The next permissible work is to identify a continuous walkable floor centerline and clearance envelope in the existing **Gate→Street→Stair** meshes, and cut only geometry demonstrably inside that envelope while preserving facades. Each floor end needs a truly matching 1.6-unit minimum passable strip, 1.6×1.6 landing, ±.25 rise join, and clearance/raycast sweep along the entire join, not merely at a named center point. The **Residential→Terrace** frontage also needs a measured side landing. On the terrain side, define a matching, sacrificial underside/rock shoulder for residence, stair and seam, then trim/bury the clearly exposed plate edges to one continuous mountain section. Test those modifications with collision sweeps and 19/12/9/oblique captures before accepting interface certification.

Do not scale this kit into production Valoria yet. Preserve the six functional originals; the `_InterfaceV1.glb` variants are review candidates, **not production-ready certified interfaces**. No change to `Valoria.unity`, `VisualWorld`, gameplay or web runtime.
