# Valoria Production Art Rollout Plan v1

Status: **PLANNED / BLOCKED ON STARTER FAMILY VISUAL PASS**  
Program: **VALORIA PRODUCTION ART SYSTEM RESET v1**

## What scales from the starter family

The five-piece starter family is not the final asset catalog. It establishes the grammar that subsequent functional buildings inherit: warm masonry with real architectural depth; structural timber rather than painted-on strips; slate roofs with thickness/eaves; recessed openings; strong bases and terrain transitions; restrained metal and heraldry; reusable trims/cornices/frames; selective wear and environmental contact.

## Building rollout

### Granero
Keep the existing functional role and plot. Reauthor toward an unmistakable storage/agricultural silhouette, loading porch, grain/sack/cart storytelling, direct field/yard relationship and a warmer timber share than military buildings.

### Cuartel
Reauthor toward heavier stone mass, guarded primary entry, defensible corners, training-yard relationship and restrained weapon/shield/heraldic cues.

### Forja
Use the workshop grammar as a base, then add a strong chimney/exhaust silhouette, soot/heat material masks, metal stock/work area, restrained warm emissive cues and a functional canopy.

### Hospital
Use civic grammar rather than industrial grammar: readable entrance, calmer court, herbs/garden/medical-supply cues, inhabited windows and cleaner circulation.

### Cantera
Integrate directly into geology with authored rock/stone transitions, cut-stone staging, hoist/work structure and no floating building-on-rock presentation.

## Bastion progression

Hero Bastion remains the quality ceiling and identity anchor. Future Bastion levels should add authored growth through towers/wings, gates/courts, roofline evolution, heraldry, civic/defensive functions and visible reconstruction-to-prosperity progression.

Do not implement Bastion progression by simply scaling the same mesh, adding primitive shells around Hero, reducing Hero to match secondary art, or returning to mountain-dominant staging.

10. Mobile/device optimization after visual target.

## Per-building gate

Each new primary building must record source BLEND identity, GLB identity, material families, zoom9/mobile BEFORE/AFTER, gameplay/hotspot/collider preservation, 12/19 after core pass, costs/credits, and whether its modules are reusable by the next asset.

## Success criterion

The system succeeds when the next building is faster and more coherent because it reuses a validated architectural/material vocabulary without becoming visibly repetitive. The goal is not five beautiful one-offs; it is a production language capable of constructing the whole Valoria reference.
