# Valoria Approved Reference Image — Review Notes

Status: **CANONICAL SUPPORTING NOTE**
Date: 2026-10-04

## Canonical image

`references/VALORIA_APPROVED_VISUAL_REFERENCE.jpg`

This is the owner-approved visual reference used to judge whether Valoria is converging toward the intended premium medieval-fantasy 4X presentation.

## What to emulate

- Bastion-first visual hierarchy without isolating the Bastion from the city.
- Strong secondary functional architecture with readable silhouettes.
- Cohesive city fabric rather than separate assets placed on terrain.
- Warm architectural stone, timber, slate/charcoal roofs and restrained blue/gold identity.
- Deliberate streets, yards, walls, stairs, foundations and architecture-ground transitions.
- Luminous, readable lighting with depth and contact shadows.
- A built, inhabited capital with environmental storytelling and a credible world beyond it.
- Enough screen-space that architecture reads as architecture, not as tiny map markers.

## What not to copy literally

- Exact building positions.
- Exact wall shape.
- Exact plaza, road or stair layout.
- Exact number of buildings or props.
- Exact density.
- A requirement that the whole city fit in one mobile viewport.

Eldoria's Flat Citadel, certified gameplay topology, progression parcels, future reserves and promoted navigable-city camera policy remain authoritative.

## Density rule

The reference is denser than Valoria needs to be.

A **somewhat less dense** Valoria is acceptable and may be preferable for gameplay readability and future progression.

It still must:
- feel premium and intentionally authored;
- avoid large unjustified dead zones;
- maintain Bastion → functional buildings → lower-city hierarchy;
- keep secondary architecture strong enough to support the Hero Bastion;
- feel like a city, not a maquette, blockout or prefab showcase.

Do not chase density by adding visual noise. Do not excuse sparse prototype-like frames by citing this density tolerance.

## Review question

Every major visual block should answer:

**Does the played frame move materially toward the cohesion, richness, hierarchy and premium presentation of the canonical reference, while preserving Eldoria's own layout and gameplay?**

If the answer is only “the prototype is cleaner,” do not promote.
