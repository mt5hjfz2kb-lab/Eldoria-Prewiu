## What the blank canvas establishes

- Hero Bastion is the summit and primary visual hierarchy.
- Urban growth is vertical and terrace-led rather than lateral sprawl.
- Dedicated work buildings occupy lower shelves.
- Upper inhabited masses remain sparse.
- Future plots are deliberately visible and empty.
- Natural territory surrounds the compact city instead of another ring of houses.
- Existing certified library assets are reused; their inherited positions are not authoritative.
- No new Tripo geometry was required to prove the composition.

## Iteration findings

The first blank-canvas render proved the direction but exposed oversized upper wall modules and a fragmented central stair.
Subsequent iterations replaced the giant wall read with buried rock/masonry transitions, narrowed and connected the processional ascent, rebuilt the surrounding landform, reduced the wide-camera procedural-board appearance, and finally broke the central ladder into an irregular stepped street with distinct civic/gate landings.
