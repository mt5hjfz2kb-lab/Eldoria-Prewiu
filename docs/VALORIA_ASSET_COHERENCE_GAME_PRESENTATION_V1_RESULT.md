## What changed and why

Canonical Hero Bastion, Aserradero, Cuartel and Granero remain. Source atlases/normals are retained while family-specific value/saturation/smoothness/normal response reduces brightness and orange/blue competition. Height/slope proxies distinguish roofs and lower Hero rock without canonical mesh surgery. Shared world-space stone/rock diffuse reduces the Hero rock's unrelated surface response; silhouette remains fused.

The detached 64x52 surrounding meadow rectangle is hidden. A shallow visual support field extends past official cameras, with low relief only outside the city kernel and radial atmospheric fade. Shared dirt/paving texel density replaces noisy procedural city ground. Canonical authored shrubs sit outside the wall with gate masks; no landscape mountain or new territory layout.

Eight small workers/guards/inhabitants and seven low-opacity sawmill smoke quads provide scale/activity at mature visual state. Motion components are bounded and visual-only, without gameplay colliders. These are proxy inhabitants, not certified production animation. Existing flags/cart/workyard vocabulary is retained rather than filling reservations.

The real Unity HUD keeps live resources, objectives, unlocks and button listeners. Separate native presentation adds restrained gold/navy framing, margins, minimum text size and responsive scaler; fixes narrow primary action sizing and decorative layout interference. Existing mockup-era decoration is bypassed on the marked canvas. No fictional UI values or image overlay replaces controls.
